For services that are not directed at children but nonetheless may appeal to them, we take additional steps to limit access to these Services based on the player's age.

For these Services, when a player indicates they are under 16 (or the applicable age in their territory), we will limit their access to certain features, such as social media and chat features, as well as certain types of notification alerts.

We also limit the processing of personal data to only what is necessary to support the internal operations of our Services.

Where applicable, we may process email addresses solely to authenticate the player account with a two-step email verification process to enable login. The data is stored internally only in hashed form after authentication and is not used for contact or other purposes.

Certain Website features can be made available to Young Players only with parental or guardian consent.

The age threshold at which parental or guardian consent is required may vary by territory and may in some jurisdictions be lower than 16 (for example, 14).

In such cases, the Young Player experience applies to players below the locally applicable age.

Parents or guardians have the possibility to later change the choices they have made about their children's use of specific features.

If you are a parent or guardian and wish to review personal data collected from your child, or have that information modified or deleted, you may contact us as described above.

If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal data contrary to the above, we will delete any personal data we have collected, unless we have a legal obligation to keep it, and terminate the child's account and/or revert them to the underage experience, as applicable.
